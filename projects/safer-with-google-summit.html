Safer with Google Summit

Privacy, safety, and security teams from every function, turned into one shared voice across more than 60 talks a year

About

The Safer with Google Summit is Google’s annual gathering for the people working across privacy, content safety, and security. It brings together teams from engineering, product, UX, research, legal, public policy, marketing, and communications to share priorities, explore common challenges, and strengthen connections across the organization.

Since 2022, I’ve served as the summit’s creative director and a key member of its organizing committee. I helped develop the themes that connected different product areas and functions around shared priorities, bringing those ideas to life through the summit’s brand and overall experience. I also contributed to executive presentations and created video content featuring the people and work behind Google’s safety efforts.

My team built a design system to support more than 60 presentations each year, including bespoke keynote decks for six executives. The templates were adopted by teams beyond the summit, giving them a consistent way to communicate their work. What began as an event design system became a broader creative resource across Google.
