Security Analysis and Reporting

Dense threat intelligence, turned into public reports for the Munich Security Conference

About

Google’s senior security analysts had uncovered new findings about cyberattacks leading up to the war in Ukraine, including newly established links between specific attacks and nation-state adversaries. I joined the project before the end of the year, helping prepare Fog of War for publication at the Munich Security Conference in February.

I worked closely with three to five senior analysts to understand their findings and what the security community needed to take away. With the report evolving until the final stages to incorporate the latest intelligence, I also worked across public policy, marketing, public relations, and legal to ensure the content was accurate and aligned with Google’s position. I translated complex attack sequences into visual explanations that made the findings accessible to non-technical audiences.

I led the design direction and created a design system used across the report, presentations, and supporting communications. That system went on to support Google’s security research reports for years, helping establish a consistent, recognizable way to communicate complex findings and reinforce Google’s leadership in cybersecurity.
