Secure AI Framework Risk Map

How AI gets attacked and defended, turned into one visual map and accepted as an industry reference

About

Google product teams, and the organizations Google works with across the industry, needed a way to find their way through the risks to AI systems and the controls for each. The security experts knew both in depth. I turned how AI systems get attacked and defended into one visual map, drawn with technical accuracy and checked directly with Google’s security engineering teams.

The map is the centre of Google’s Secure AI Framework (SAIF). It draws an AI system in four layers (data, infrastructure, model, and application) running from how a model is made to how it’s used. On it sit 15 risks, from data poisoning and model theft to prompt injection and rogue agent actions. For each one, the map shows where the risk gets in, where it shows up, and where it can be stopped, and links it to 24 controls. A guided tour on saif.google walks through the risks one at a time.

In 2025 Google donated SAIF’s data, the risk map included, to the Coalition for Secure AI, an OASIS Open project whose members include Amazon, Anthropic, IBM, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and OpenAI. CoSAI now maintains it as the CoSAI Risk Map: a common language for AI security across the industry.
