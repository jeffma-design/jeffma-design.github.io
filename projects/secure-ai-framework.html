Systems & Information Design

Secure AI Framework Risk Map

How AI gets attacked and defended, turned into one visual map and accepted as an industry reference

About

Google product teams, and the organizations Google works with across the industry, needed a way through the risks to AI systems and the controls for each. The security experts knew both in depth. I directed the design of one visual map of how AI systems get attacked and defended: I set what it had to show and how it should read, a designer on my team drew it, and together we checked it for technical accuracy with Google’s security engineering teams.

The map is the centre of Google’s Secure AI Framework (SAIF). For every risk, from data poisoning to prompt injection, it shows where the risk gets in, where it shows up, and where it can be stopped.

Role
Design direction, stakeholder validation
Scope
Four layers, 15 risks, 24 controls
Used by
Google product teams; Privacy, Safety & Security; Coalition for Secure AI (CoSAI)

saif.google · CoSAI
What changed

Donated the risk map to the Coalition for Secure AI, where it is maintained as a shared reference across the industry.
