Secure AI Framework Risk Map

How AI gets attacked and defended, turned into one visual map and accepted as an industry reference

About

The map is the centerpiece of Google’s Secure AI Framework (SAIF). For every risk, from data poisoning to prompt injection, it shows where the risk gets in, where it shows up, and where it can be stopped.

Google’s product areas and the organizations it works with across the industry needed a shared mental model and inventory of the risks to AI systems and the controls for addressing them. That expertise existed across the organization, but it wasn’t yet captured in a single, shared view. I directed the design of a visual map to show how AI systems get attacked and defended.

The design team went through multiple iterations to achieve technical accuracy with Google’s security engineering teams. Working through the map together helped the engineering teams validate not just individual details, but how the entire system fit together—confirming connections, exposing vulnerabilities, and identifying where controls and remediations belonged.
