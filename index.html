For twenty years, I’ve been the person experts call when they need to sell a lifestyle to a customer, explain technology to non-technical people, or report on the health of a corporation.

My observation

The closer you are to an idea, the harder it can be to explain.

It’s called the curse of knowledge. Once you know something deeply, what feels obvious to you loses everyone else. An outside eye can help you see what to explain, what to leave out, and how to move audiences.

  4. Shape

    I tune your story for the people hearing it.

    Then I design what carries it.
